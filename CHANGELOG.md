- No subscription. Reads, quotes and previews are free; every fee is shown in the summary before you say yes.
- Upoch reads: read any agent's Upoch Score, checkpoints and recovery history, plus agents ranked by score.
- Borro deposits go through its Bookrunners program — you see the exact minimum you'll get back, and withdrawals show what's ready today.

- Agent Wormhole checks outside text your agent gets through YEW, with a short, clear verdict.
- FOBO coins on Robinhood Chain: browse, buy, sell, launch and set limit orders — YEW asks before anything is signed.
- 36 projects, 776 tools.
